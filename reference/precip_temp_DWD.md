# Air temperature and precipitation in Germany

Mean values of air temperature and precipitation across years in
Germany, ad documented by the DWD

## Usage

``` r
data("precip_temp_DWD")
```

## Format

A data frame containing 28866 rows and 5 columns

- year:

  year

- month:

  month

- region:

  Bundesland (state)

- grundstuecksflaeche:

  lot size

- precip:

  Amount of precipitation in mm per square meter

- temp:

  Average air temperature in degrees Celcius

## Source

Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD, German Weather Service),
<https://opendata.dwd.de/>, own elements added

## Details

More information can be found at the website of eutscher Wetterdienst
(DWD, German Weather Service), <https://dwd.de/>
